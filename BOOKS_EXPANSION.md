JEUFREX Books expansion

10 original serialized stories were added to the repository as data and reader logic.
Each story is structured as 3 chapters with exactly 50 generated page records per chapter (150 page records per story).
Chapter 1 is published; Chapters 2 and 3 are marked as coming soon.
Genres: K-Drama, Korku / Gerilim, Türk Komedi, Macera, Fantastik, Bilim Kurgu, Gizem, Spor, Tarih, Günlük Yaşam.
The reader supports chapter selection, page turning, keyboard arrows and a 50-page progress model.
